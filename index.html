Digos City, Philippines · Mar 2020 to Jun 2021

Center for Law and Graduate Studies, Cor Jesu College

Problem

A 3,000 m² three-storey academic building had to rise just 182 meters from the Digos Fault Line and survive severe seismic forces without an inflated budget.

Approach

Modeled the structure in ETABS at a peak ground acceleration of 0.40g to 0.50g, then placed Vierendeel trusses at high-stress points to dissipate seismic energy instead of relying on costly composite sections. Designed the foundations in VisualFoundation and produced the construction drawings in AutoCAD.

Result

A structure compliant with the NSCP and NBCP, delivered with complete cost estimates, BOQ and BOM, and coordinated with architects, contractors and suppliers through construction.

Digos City, Philippines · Aug 2021 to Feb 2023

Norbert Building Retrofit, Cor Jesu College

Problem

A six-storey building was unsafe for occupancy after a 6.3-magnitude earthquake. Site investigation found construction shortcuts: undersized rebar, missing wall stiffeners, incorrect reinforcement spacing and a hollow beam.

Approach

Led the damage assessment and ETABS analysis, then designed a retrofit built on concrete jacketing, Vierendeel trusses for seismic energy absorption, and lightweight AAC blocks and PU panels to cut building weight. Every defect was documented with photographs.

Result

The original contractor agreed to cover remediation costs based on the documented deviations. The retrofit was delivered on a tight budget by reusing scaffolding and shoring across phases, tracked with Gantt charts and S-curves.

Approach
