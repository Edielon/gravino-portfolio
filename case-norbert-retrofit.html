02

Norbert Building Retrofit, Cor Jesu College

Digos City, Philippines · 2021 to 2023

  • 6storeys retrofitted
  • 6.3magnitude earthquake

Problem

A six-storey building was unsafe for occupancy after a 6.3-magnitude earthquake. Site investigation found construction shortcuts: undersized rebar, missing wall stiffeners, incorrect reinforcement spacing and a hollow beam.

Approach

Led the damage assessment and ETABS analysis, then designed a retrofit built on concrete jacketing, Vierendeel trusses for seismic energy absorption, and lightweight AAC blocks and PU panels to cut building weight. Every defect was documented with photographs.

Result

The original contractor agreed to cover remediation costs based on the documented deviations. The retrofit was delivered on a tight budget by reusing scaffolding and shoring across phases, tracked with Gantt charts and S-curves.

Tools

ETABS · AutoCAD · SketchUp · Gantt / S-curve scheduling
